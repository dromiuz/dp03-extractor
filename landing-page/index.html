The recorder captured the song. The confusion came later.

Tascam Portastudios are great for getting ideas down. The harder part is what happens after months or years of saved sessions.

  • You know the song is on the card, but the files don’t make sense.
  • You’re not sure what’s safe to touch.
  • Older projects start to feel risky.
  • You want normal WAVs out without menu-diving and guesswork.

Who it’s for

  • Tascam Portastudio users with old SD-card projects
  • People with years of half-finished songs
  • Home recordists who want safer desktop visibility
  • Anyone tired of guessing what’s really on the card

What it isn’t

  • Not a DAW
  • Not mastering software
  • Not cloud collaboration
  • Not a replacement for the recorder itself

Why I built it

The recording part was fine. The project-management part wasn’t. I wanted a desktop tool that made Tascam sessions clearer, safer, and easier to extract without pretending to be a full music-production environment.

FAQ

Does this replace my Tascam recorder?

No. Your recorder still does the recording. This app is the desktop utility layer around your projects.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The normal use case is local and offline.

Is it a DAW?

No. It’s a focused utility for inspecting Tascam project data and exporting usable audio.

Is it safe for old projects?

It’s designed to help you inspect projects before acting on them, but you should still keep backups of important cards and images.
